## 6. Trends & Implications

### For Patients in Underserved Regions
- Only **17.3% of prosthetic trials are actively recruiting** — limited ability to find nearby trials
- **No trials are sited in WV, KY, or MS** — patients must travel to major metros
- **Phase 3 trials are rare** — evidence for next-gen devices is lacking

### For Policy & Advocacy
- **Medicaid non-expansion in WV and MS** exacerbates access barriers
- **Industry sponsorship at 16.6%** suggests room for public-private partnerships
- **NA/Unknown phase at 52.4%** highlights need for better clinical trial reporting transparency

### For Research Community
- **Mobile/digital trial designs** could reach underserved populations without requiring local infrastructure
- **Telemedicine-enabled CPO services** could bridge the 130–200 mile gap
- **Community-based participatory research** should be prioritized in high-amputation regions
