## Key Conclusions

1. **Zero prosthetic/orthotic (CPO) providers** exist within 30 km of any of the three target regions.
2. **Nearest CPO is 130–200+ miles away**, requiring 3+ hours of driving each way.
3. **WV and MS have not expanded Medicaid**, creating financial barriers to prosthetic care.
4. **Mississippi Delta has the highest amputation rate in the U.S.** — yet has zero trial sites or CPO providers.
5. **No prosthetic clinical trial sites** exist in WV, KY, or MS despite high amputation rates.
6. **Late-stage trials (Phase 3-4) are critically scarce** — only 92 out of 2,222 studies (4.2%).
7. **29.9% of trials have unknown status**, indicating poor follow-up and data integrity issues.
