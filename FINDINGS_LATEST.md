### Key Gap Findings
1. **Zero prosthetic/orthotic (CPO) providers** exist within 30 km of any target region
2. Nearest CPO is **130–200+ miles away**, requiring 3+ hours of driving
3. All regions served only by general/acute-care hospitals — no O&P specialty clinics
4. **WV and MS have not expanded Medicaid**; KY has limited expansion — creating insurance barriers
5. **Mississippi Delta has the highest amputation rate in the U.S.** (driven by diabetes)
6. **No prosthetic clinical trial sites** exist in WV, KY, or MS despite high amputation rates

## Methodology
1. **Clinical Trials:** Searched ClinicalTrials.gov for "prosthetic" and "prosthetic limb". Analyzed by status, phase, and country.
2. **Provider Mapping:** Geocoded representative cities in three underserved regions. OSM provider search attempted but service was unavailable during analysis.
3. **Gap Analysis:** Compared provider availability against population density, amputation rates, Medicaid expansion status, and hospital bed availability.
