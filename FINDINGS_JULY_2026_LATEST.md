## Key Findings

1. **Zero prosthetic/orthotic (CPO) providers** exist within 30 km of any of the three target regions.
2. The nearest CPO is **130–200+ miles away**, requiring 3+ hours of driving — often infeasible for elderly or mobility-impaired patients.
3. **WV and MS have not expanded Medicaid**, creating additional financial barriers to prosthetic care.
4. **The Mississippi Delta has the highest amputation rate in the U.S.** yet has zero local prosthetic providers.
5. **No prosthetic clinical trial sites** exist in WV, KY, or MS despite documented high amputation rates —clinical research is entirely disconnected from the highest-need populations.
6. Of all prosthetic trials, only **3.1% are Phase 3**; the vast majority are observational (58.6% NA), indicating a research gap in device efficacy and intervention-focused studies.

---

## Recommended Actions

1. **Mobile CPO clinics** — deploy traveling prosthetic/orthotic fitting services to Beckley, Hazard, and Greenville.
2. **Telehealth prosthetic consultations** — virtual fitting and follow-up to reduce travel burden.
3. **Clinical trial recruitment** — establish trial sites in WV, KY, and MS to include underserved populations in prosthetic research.
4. **Medicaid advocacy** — push for Medicaid expansion in WV and MS to cover prosthetic services.
5. **Training pipelines** — fund O&P residency placements in rural Appalachia and the Delta to build local workforce.
