### Key Observations
- **Phase 3 trials are critically scarce** — only 30 of 655 (4.6%) are Phase 3, reflecting limited late-stage evidence for prosthetic interventions.
- **Nearly half (42.1%) are Completed** — a mature research field with significant published evidence but limited ongoing recruitment.
- **Only 17.3% are actively recruiting** — opportunities exist for patient participation but also for new recruitment-focused trials.
- **Academic sponsors dominate** (76.5%), indicating strong university-led research but limited industry commercial investment.
- **The US leads globally** in prosthetic clinical research volume, but international contributions (France, Denmark, Germany) are substantial.

### Critical Findings

#### 1. Zero Prosthetic/Orthotic (CPO) Providers Within 30 km
- All five target locations have **zero certified prosthetists/orthotists** within a 30 km radius.
- Nearest CPO providers are **130–200+ miles away**, requiring 3+ hours of driving.

#### 2. Medicaid Expansion Status
- **West Virginia**: Has NOT expanded Medicaid (as of 2026 analysis period)
- **Kentucky**: Limited Medicaid expansion; coverage gaps persist in rural eastern counties
- **Mississippi**: Has NOT expanded Medicaid; Delta region has highest uninsured rates in the nation

#### 3. Amputation Rates
- **Mississippi Delta**: Has the **highest amputation rate in the United States**, driven primarily by diabetes and peripheral vascular disease.
- **Eastern Kentucky**: High amputation rates due to diabetes, obesity, and limited preventive care access.
- **Rural West Virginia**: Elevated amputation rates; diabetes prevalence exceeds national average.

#### 4. Clinical Trial Site Deserts
- **Zero prosthetic clinical trial sites** exist in West Virginia, Kentucky, or Mississippi despite high amputation rates and demonstrated need.
- Patients in these regions cannot participate in cutting-edge prosthetic research without traveling hundreds of miles.

### Recommended Actions

1. **Deploy mobile CPO units** to rotate through Beckley, Pikeville, Ashland, Greenville, and Clarksdale on a scheduled basis
2. **Establish telehealth CPO consultations** to reduce travel burden for adjustment and fitting
3. **Target these regions for clinical trial recruitment** — no trials are currently sited here despite high need
4. **Advocate for Medicaid expansion** in WV and MS to improve insurance coverage for prosthetic devices
5. **Create a prosthetics fellowship pipeline** placing resident-trained CPOs in underserved regions for 2-year service commitments
