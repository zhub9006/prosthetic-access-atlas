### Critical Gap Findings

1. **Zero CPO providers within 30 km** of any target region — all five locations are prosthetic care deserts.
2. **Nearest CPO is 130–200+ miles away**, requiring 3+ hours of driving for basic prosthetic services.
3. **WV and MS have not expanded Medicaid**; high uninsured rates limit prosthetic device access even further.
4. **Mississippi Delta has the highest amputation rate in the U.S.**, driven by diabetes and peripheral vascular disease.
5. **No prosthetic clinical trial sites** in WV, KY, or MS despite demonstrated high need.

## PART 5: RECOMMENDED INTERVENTIONS

1. **Deploy mobile O&P clinics** rotating through Beckley, Pikeville, Ashland, Greenville, and Clarksdale on a scheduled basis
2. **Establish telehealth CPO consultations** to reduce travel burden for adjustment and fitting appointments
3. **Target these regions for clinical trial recruitment** — no trials are currently sited here despite high need, creating opportunities for patient participation
4. **Expand Medicaid** in WV and MS to cover prosthetic devices and rehabilitation services
5. **Create a prosthetics fellowship pipeline** placing resident-trained CPOs in underserved regions for 2-year service commitments
6. **Leverage 3D printing technology** (NCT06938087 model) to decentralize prosthetic fabrication and reduce cost barriers
7. **Partner with rural hospitals** to add O&P competency training for local providers and emergency department staff
