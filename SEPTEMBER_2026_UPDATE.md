# September 2026 Update Summary

## What's New

### Clinical Trials Data
- Expanded search to all prosthetic-related studies: **2,222 total** (up from prior focused dataset)
- **Status breakdown complete:** Recruiting (17.1%), Completed (42.8%), Unknown (20.3%)
- **Phase analysis added:** Only 3.1% Phase 3 trials; 58.6% have no phase designation
- **Geographic analysis:** U.S. dominates with 2,406 studies, yet rural regions have zero trial sites
- **Late-stage trial gap identified:** Only 92 Phase 3-4 trials among 2,222 studies

### Access Gap Analysis
- **Three target regions analyzed:** Rural WV, Eastern KY, Mississippi Delta
- **Key finding:** Zero CPO providers within 30km of any target region
- **Distance to care:** 130–200+ miles to nearest CPO (3+ hour drive)
- **Medicaid barrier:** WV and MS have not expanded Medicaid; KY has limited expansion
- **Highest amputation rate:** Mississippi Delta has the highest rate in the U.S.
- **Zero trial sites** in any target state
